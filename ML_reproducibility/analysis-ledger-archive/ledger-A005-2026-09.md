### A-005 — TD-DFT reference run: higher level of theory vs structure-only ML (closes Referee 1)

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Question:** Referee 1 (DD rejection) argued A-001 only tested a semi-empirical level
  (GFN2-xTB/sTDA) and that a proper TD-DFT gap with a range-separated functional would correlate
  much better with experiment (r > 0.95) and beat structure-only ML. Does it, on this dataset?
- **Source:** `code/ref1_tddft_gap.py` → `data/ref1_tddft_gap.json`.
  TD-DFT run: `data/tddft_reference_231.csv` (+ `data/tddft_reference_timing.json`), produced by
  `tddft_server_package/run_tddft.py` on the frozen 231-row set. Same experimental labels, SMILES,
  NTO features and Bemis–Murcko scaffold GroupKFold(5) folds as A-001 — every row is comparable.
- **Method:** ORCA 6, **wB97X-D4/def2-SVP, RIJCOSX, TDA, nroots 8, triplets true, gas-phase
  vertical** at the ground-state geometry. ΔE_ST = E(S1) − E(T1). **231/231 molecules usable,
  0 failures, all jobs TERMINATED NORMALLY, all states parsed, no inverted gaps.**
  Cost: 604.5 core-hours (8 procs, 76.2 h wall), median 18.2 min/molecule, slowest 1.13 h,
  wall ∝ atoms^1.89. RF 400 trees, seed 0. Paired ΔMAE: 2000-resample bootstrap on identical
  folds + Wilcoxon signed-rank on paired absolute errors. In-fold linear calibration only (no leak).
- **Result:**

- **Interpretation:** The referee's expectation fails on the level it points to, and the run
  answers the objection completely. The raw TD-DFT gap is the **worst** predictor in the study —
  MAE 0.654 eV, six times the mean baseline, R² = −16.3 — because TDA with a range-separated hybrid
  overestimates the exchange splitting by a fixed +0.654 eV on this set, more than triple the
  +0.221 eV sTDA offset. Correlation does improve with level of theory: Pearson r rises 0.322 → 0.432
  and Spearman ρ 0.237 → 0.377 going from sTDA to TD-DFT. It does not reach the r > 0.95 the referee
  cites, which comes from single-protocol literature sets, not a 231-molecule multi-source
  compilation. Once the systematic offset is removed — by an in-fold linear calibration that needs
  experimental training labels, so it is no longer a zero-parameter physics predictor — the TD-DFT
  gap reaches MAE 0.099 eV, better than the mean baseline (paired ΔMAE +0.0085, CI excludes zero)
  and better than the calibrated sTDA gap (0.104), but still not better than the structure-only
  random forest (B4 vs M1: ΔMAE −0.0075, CI crosses zero). Added as one feature to the random
  forest, the TD-DFT gap gives the first physics contribution in the study that clears a paired
  test: Morgan + TD-DFT MAE 0.087 vs Morgan 0.091 (ΔMAE +0.0039, CI [+0.0004, +0.0071], Wilcoxon
  p = 0.016); NTO + TD-DFT 0.092 vs 0.096 similarly. The effect is small — a 4 % MAE reduction for
  604 core-hours — and it did not appear at the sTDA level (A-001: NTO 0.096 → 0.097, inside CI).
  So the honest three-part answer to Referee 1: (1) used raw, a higher level of theory is
  dramatically worse, not better, driven by a large systematic offset; (2) it does track experiment
  better in rank and, after calibration, in MAE, but not well enough to replace the ML model;
  (3) supplied to the model as a feature it adds a small but statistically resolved gain. The weak
  sTDA–TD-DFT agreement (r 0.46, +0.43 eV mean shift) is itself evidence that vertical ΔE_ST is
  method-sensitive at the 0.1–0.6 eV scale the screening problem lives on.
- **Caveats:** (i) Gas-phase **vertical** at the ground-state geometry — no solvent, no
  excited-state relaxation; adiabatic/solvated values shift the offset (A-004 P6: vertical vs
  CAM-B3LYP adiabatic MAE 0.195 eV). The +0.654 eV bias is specific to wB97X-D4/def2-SVP/TDA.
  (ii) def2-SVP is a small basis; def2-TZVP would move the absolute gap but the systematic-offset
  argument is basis-robust for a range-separated hybrid. (iii) TDA raises triplet energies relative
  to full TD-DFT, widening ΔE_ST — full-TDDFT triplets would lower the offset but are prone to
  triplet instability; TDA was the deliberate, stable choice. (iv) Calibration in B2/B4 is a
  per-fold degree-1 fit on ~185 training molecules; it removes offset and scale, nothing higher
  order. (v) M6/M5 gains (ΔMAE ≈ 0.003–0.004 eV) are real by the paired test but small against the
  0.072 eV label-noise floor (A-003) — a genuine, bounded, not transformative improvement.
  (vi) 212/231 scaffolds are singletons, so GroupKFold ≈ random split here (A-002 P3); this applies
  identically to every row and does not change the ordering.
