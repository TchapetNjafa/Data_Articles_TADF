\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies}
In preparing this work the authors used AI assistants (Claude by Anthropic --- Sonnet
4.5, accessed May~2026; Sonnet~5 and Opus~5, accessed September~2026 --- and ChatGPT by
OpenAI). Their use covered literature triage, \LaTeX{} formatting and consistency
checking, drafting and revision of manuscript text, and writing the analysis scripts
whose outputs are reported here --- among them the paired equivalence and nested
cross-validation tests, the enrichment and label-precision audits, and the
pretrained-encoder baseline. The deposited analysis ledger records the provenance of
every value. The AI assistants are not authors and made no independent scientific
judgement; all numerical values, computational methods and interpretations were checked
against the deposited data and approved by the authors, who take full responsibility
for them.

% Data availability
\section*{Data availability}
All experimental data, descriptor tables, trained models, and analysis code are
openly available on Zenodo under DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17436069 (CC-BY-4.0 license).
The deposit includes: (1)~the experimental \deltaest\ corpus and the
\num{231}-molecule benchmark set (\num{212} scaffolds); (2)~the \num{35} NTO spatial
descriptors and the structure-only Morgan and RDKit feature tables; (3)~the DFT
functional benchmark and the CAM-B3LYP vertical-versus-adiabatic and triplet-manifold
data (\num{14} emitters); (4)~the trained random-forest models, SHAP attributions, and
validation outputs (scaffold and Butina-cluster cross-validation, label-permutation
test, capacity tests); (5)~the heuristic \num{22194}-molecule library with the
conformal calibration script and its interval widths---no ranked candidate list is
deposited, because at this accuracy the intervals do not support molecule-level
selection; and (6)~all analysis and figure-generation
scripts that regenerate every reported number.
