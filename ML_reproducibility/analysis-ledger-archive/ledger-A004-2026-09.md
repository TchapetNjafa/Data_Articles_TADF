### A-004 — Referee 3 points 4–7: verdicts from existing data

- **Date:** 2026-08-29
- **Question:** Are Referee 3's remaining four objections (expansion collapse, virtual-shortlist
  prediction intervals, vertical-only descriptors, INVEST discussion) fixable, and by what?
- **Source:** `data/expansion_stress_test.json` · `data/shortlist_top100.csv` ·
  `data/multi_criteria_triage.json` · `data/enrichment_curve.json` ·
  `data/vertical_vs_adiabatic_comparison.csv` + `data/adiabatic_validation_stats.json` ·
  `data/ist_candidates.json`. No new computation; all values read from committed outputs.
- **Result:**

  **P4 expansion (231 → 728).** Baseline n=231: MAE 0.098 eV, R² 0.182, ρ 0.336. Expanded n=728:
  MAE 0.124 eV, R² −0.008, ρ 0.162. Multi-report subset n=465: MAE 0.110 eV, R² 0.052, ρ 0.240.
  Composition of the 624 candidate additions: 127 duplicates of training molecules, 497 usable new.

  **P5 virtual shortlist.** Top-100 of 22,194: predicted ΔE_ST spans 0.023–0.053 eV; mean 90%
  split-conformal interval width 0.356 eV; lowest interval bound −0.155 eV; **100% of the 100
  shortlisted molecules have a prediction interval whose lower bound is negative**, i.e. every
  interval spans an unphysical inverted gap. Mean ratio of interval width to predicted value 7.6.

  **P6 vertical vs adiabatic.** n=27 points / 14 molecules, sTDA-xTB vertical vs CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVP
  excited-state-optimised: MAE 0.195 eV, RMSE 0.245 eV, mean bias +0.071 eV, Pearson r² 0.704,
  ρ 0.711, linear fit slope 1.002 / intercept 0.070 eV, max deviation 0.548 eV (2CzTPE, gas).

  **P7 INVEST.** 66 literature IST records, 61 unique names, 25 DOIs, ΔE_ST −0.432 to −0.010 eV
  (mean −0.112). **`n_with_smiles` = 0** — no structures resolved. Of the 5 IST molecules present in
  the corpus, the model predicts the wrong sign for **5/5** (fraction 1.00), predicting +0.115 to
  +0.184 eV where experiment is negative. Current text footprint: 5 mentions in results, 4 in SI.

- **Interpretation:** Two of the four are conceded, one is partly defensible, one must be cut.
  **P4 — concede and promote.** Tripling the corpus makes prediction worse, not better, and the
  multi-report subset recovers only half the loss. This is the strongest evidence in the paper that
  the limit is the label, not the model or the sample size, and it directly refutes Referee 1's
  "just use a bigger dataset" reflex. It belongs in the main text as a result, not buried in SI as a
  stress test.
  **P5 — the referee is right and the claim must go.** Every shortlisted molecule's interval covers
  a negative gap, and the interval is 7.6× the predicted value. A shortlist of specific molecules
  cannot be defended at that resolution. Drop the top-100 table and the 22,194-molecule screen as a
  *prediction* claim. What survives is the enrichment measurement on labelled data
  (base rate 0.494; precision 0.652 at 10% and 0.783 at 20% of the ranked list, enrichment 1.32× and
  1.59×) — that is a ranking statement on held-out molecules with known answers, which is a different
  and defensible claim. Reframe the virtual library as a demonstration that conformal intervals
  *refuse* to support molecule-level selection at this accuracy — a negative methodological result
  consistent with the paper's thesis.
  **P6 — partly defensible, and the defence is quantitative.** The referee is right that MAE 0.195 eV
  is too large to wave away, and it exceeds the model's own error. But slope 1.002 with intercept
  0.070 eV and ρ = 0.711 do support the rank-preserving reading for the 14 molecules tested: the
  discrepancy is dominated by an additive offset plus scatter, not by reordering. State both halves —
  the offset is systematic, the residual scatter (RMSE 0.245 eV, r² 0.704 leaves ~30% of variance
  unexplained) is not, and 14 molecules is too few to settle it. Say plainly that ground-state
  vertical descriptors are a documented approximation whose cost is bounded but not negligible.
  **P7 — cut.** The referee's physics is correct: single-determinant sTDA under Aufbau occupation
  cannot produce an inverted gap, and the data confirm the consequence — 5/5 wrong sign. The IST
  section makes a claim the method cannot support. Remove it from results and SI (9 mentions) and
  replace with one sentence stating the method's domain of validity excludes Hund's-rule-violating
  systems. Keeping it invites the same rejection at the next venue.
- **Caveats:** (i) P4's comparison is not like-for-like on chemical diversity: the 497 new molecules
  come from a different resolution pipeline than the curated 231, so "label noise" and "wider chemical
  space" are confounded, exactly as `expansion_stress_test.json` notes. Do not attribute the collapse
  to label noise alone. (ii) P4 baseline MAE reads 0.098 eV here versus 0.096 eV elsewhere — different
  script, same model family; pin one value before printing. (iii) P6 rests on 14 molecules / 27 points
  and on CAM-B3LYP being the reference; a different functional shifts the offset (functional spread
  0.296 eV mean, `data/ceiling_decomposition.json`). (iv) P7's sign test uses only 5 molecules — the
  physics argument, not the 5/5 count, is what carries the conclusion. (v) `ist_candidates.json` has
  zero resolved SMILES, so no IST claim of any kind can be made from that file.
- **Claim link:** C4 REVISED (enrichment survives, molecule-level shortlist does not); new C7
  "expansion degrades accuracy — the ceiling is the label"; C8 "method excludes inverted-gap systems".
